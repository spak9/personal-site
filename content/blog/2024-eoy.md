I intended to give an update midway last year as per my 
[last post](https://www.stevenpak.me/blog/2023-midway-goals/), but it seems like the end of 2024 has already arrived.
A lot has changed!

## The Delta
Oh, where to start... Let's just start with the most consequential change: I moved to New York City.


### I: Growing, or lack thereof
So, I _was_ a mobile forensics software engineer at [ManTech](https://www.mantech.com/) around February of last year. 
I was doing good work and was definitely growing confident in my ability to perform in my role, 
but I wasn't particularly happy with my growth as an _engineer_. Not to toot my own horn, but while I was growing quickly 
as a prominent member of my team, I had noticed that I reached an asymptote in my technical abilities. I was performing
the same tasks day in and day out and there wasn't much deviation, therefore the technical challenge from the work 
itself slowly dried up. In addition to the evergrowing plainness of work, I wasn't receiving a consistent form of
mentorship, a property that now looking back on in retrospect, is even more important than I gave it credit for.
Not to say that I didn't have a mentor, I actually had an amazing mentor in whom I really respected both technically, but 
more importantly, as a person.
